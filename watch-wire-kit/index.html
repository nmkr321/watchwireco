01Why this exists

The whole watch world, in one swipe

Watch news is scattered across brand sites, auction catalogues, forums and celebrity feeds. The Watch Wire pulls it into one place and gets to the point: what happened, what it cost, and why it matters to people who love watches.

What it is

A daily luxury watch news page. Releases, auctions, prices, celebrity collections and brand stories, told fast and checked first.

What it is not

Not a dealer. Not paid hype. Not price predictions or "investment advice". Not reposts of other creators' content.

The promise

Every post has a real source. Every number is checked. If something is a rumour, we say so.

02Wordmark + monogram

One wordmark. One monogram.

THE WATCH WIRE
Wordmark · Oswald 700 · news slides
WW
Monogram · Playfair Display 600 · story slides + icon

Anatomy

  • THE WATCH: Oswald 700, ivory.
  • WIRE: Oswald 700, champagne. The only colour in the mark.
  • Always uppercase, letter-spacing 0.06em.
  • Story slides use the serif version: THE WATCH WIRE in Playfair Display, all ivory.

Laws

  • WIRE is always champagne on news slides. Nothing else in the mark is.
  • The wordmark sits top-left on news slides. The monogram sits top-centre on story slides.
  • Never both marks at the top of the same slide.
  • No drawn logo file is needed. The type is the logo.

08Champagne, signal only

One champagne moment per slide

Champagne is used for

  • The topic tag (AUCTIONS, MARKET…)
  • The second line of a news headline
  • The key number on a data slide
  • WIRE in the wordmark
  • One key fact in a story sentence
  • The follow button on the last slide

Champagne is never used for

  • Full backgrounds or big fills
  • Body text or long sentences
  • Text on ivory (it fails contrast)
  • Decorative borders and frames
  • Two separate signals on one slide (the tag + wordmark pair is the one exception)
  • Tinting photos

13Voice + headlines

Confident. Insider. Checked.

Write like a well-connected collector telling a friend the news: short, specific and sure of the facts. Big numbers and names do the work, so the copy doesn't need hype.

Write like this

Swiss watch exports rose 9.1% in October. Fifth month in a row. Spotted: a $460K Jacob & Co. at the ATP gala. Rolex Started As A London Import Company. Here's How It Became Rolex.

Not like this

The watch market is EXPLODING 🚀🚀 This watch will DOUBLE in value, buy now!! You won't BELIEVE what this celebrity wore…

Always

Name the source for every number. Give dates for prices. Say "reportedly" for anything unconfirmed. One idea per slide.

Never

Price predictions or investment advice. Clickbait that the slides don't pay off. Emojis in headlines. Reposting another creator's slides.

Calls to action

Calm and direct. "Vote in the comments." "Swipe for the full list." "Follow @watchwireco for daily watch news."

14Photos + credits

Press photos, always credited

Use

  • Official brand press images and auction-house lot photos.
  • Event photos from agencies or the event's press room.
  • Your own photos, which need no permission.
  • Clean product shots on dark or neutral backgrounds for Template A.

Rules

  • Credit line on every slide with a photo: "Image: Brand" or "Image: Phillips". Smoke, 16px, bottom-right.
  • Press photos are logged as "permission needed" in the agents' asset list. Publishing still carries some risk.
  • No screenshots of other creators' posts and no images with someone else's watermark.
  • Don't flip, recolour or AI-edit a watch. Collectors notice.

16Spacing · 8pt grid

Eight. Sixteen. Twenty-four. Forty-eight.

  • Slide margin is 48px on all four sides. Every slide.
  • Tag to headline: 24px. Headline to meta line: 32px.
  • Only multiples of 8. Values like 22px or 30px are not used for layout.
  • Optical tweaks inside type are allowed.
