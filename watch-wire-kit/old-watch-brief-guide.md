# Brand guide

## Brand name

The Watch Brief

## Audience

Watch enthusiasts: collectors and hobbyists who follow brands, releases, and the
market. They want short, accurate updates on what is happening in the watch
world and why it matters to them.

## Voice

- Clear
- Confident
- Factual, no hype

Write like this: "Swiss watch exports rose 9.1% in October. That's five months of growth in a row."

Do not sound like this: "The watch market is EXPLODING 🚀 you won't believe this!!"

## Colors

- Primary: Gold #C9A45C
- Background: Near-black #0E0E10
- Text: Off-white #F5F2EA
- Accent: Muted grey #8A8A8F

## Typography

- Headline: Playfair Display (fallback: Georgia, serif)
- Body: Inter (fallback: Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif)
- Labels or numbers: Inter Bold (fallback: Helvetica Bold, Arial Bold)

## Logo rules

Text-only; no logo. Show "The Watch Brief" as a small wordmark in Inter, in
gold, at the bottom of each slide.

## Imagery

- Use: big numbers, simple charts, and clean typographic layouts.
- Treatment: high contrast on the dark background, thin gold rules and borders, source line on data slides.
- Avoid: stock photos, brand logos or product photos we do not have permission to use, busy collages.

## Visual direction

Dark luxury: quiet, premium, and editorial. Lots of space, one idea per slide.

## Content rules

Always:

- Name the source for every number.
- Keep one idea per slide.

Never:

- Use hype, clickbait, or price predictions.
- State unconfirmed claims as fact.

## CTA style

Calm and direct. Example: "Follow for the monthly watch market check-in."
