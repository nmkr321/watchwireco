# Lessons from the approver
The editor reads this before every post. Written automatically from rejections; newest last.

- [WOULD YOU RATHER] Before choosing a pair, list at least three photo-compliant candidates per price tier. If one fails the photo or price check, pick the next from that list.
- [WOULD YOU RATHER] If no pair fits the same-date 10% price window, widen the search to other library dates or brands, then choose the closest compliant pair.
- [CELEBRITY] Use only stories whose full text loads from the source and that a second independent outlet also reports; drop paywalled or unconfirmed ones.
- [CELEBRITY] Skip any celebrity who is a current brand ambassador or paid partner of the watch's maker; choose a non-endorsing wearer.
- [CELEBRITY] Name a watch reference only when a fetched source states it; if the source is silent or blocked, pick another story.
- [CELEBRITY] If the best lead fails the checks, search further celebrity stories until one passes, rather than submitting the closest weak lead.
- [CELEBRITY] Confirm the celebrity is photographed wearing the watch at a named event, using a fetched photo caption or article, before drafting.
- [CELEBRITY] Drop leads whose story or event date is more than 7 days before today, and check dates against a fetched page.
- [WOULD YOU RATHER] Before submitting, check each slide against the approver's recurring rejections in state/lessons.md and fix matches first.
- [WOULD YOU RATHER] Before rendering, check every watch photo for leftover dark boxes or halos, and scale each watch to the same size and height across slides.
- [WOULD YOU RATHER] Shorten long reference numbers to the model name when near card edges; keep all text clear of card and margin edges.
- [WOULD YOU RATHER] After swapping any watch in a draft, update every slide, price label and caption that names it, then re-check the 7-day and 10% rules.
- [WOULD YOU RATHER] Before choosing a pair, check the posted-history records for each reference; any match within 7 days is disqualified.
- [WOULD YOU RATHER] Place every watch photo on the template's plain black background with no grey or white boxes, matching the other slides before rendering.
- [WOULD YOU RATHER] After any text change, check that the image credit and footer stay clear of the content block and the slide edges.
- [WOULD YOU RATHER] After rendering, view each slide PNG for grey panels behind watches; a passing validator does not catch them.
- [WOULD YOU RATHER] When choosing a pair, prefer watch photos that already have transparent or solid black backgrounds, so no panel fix is needed.
- [WOULD YOU RATHER] Confirm each photo's source page shows the exact reference named on its slides; if not, swap the item. Footers credit only that item's source.
- [WOULD YOU RATHER] Give paired columns the same line count so prices align; shorten the longer label rather than letting it wrap.
- [WOULD YOU RATHER] On two-watch slides, the footer credits each watch's photo source with its exact credit line, kept short and clear of the page number.
