# Lessons from the approver
The editor reads this before every post. Written automatically from rejections; newest last.

- [CELEBRITY COLLECTION] Make count words in cover and sub-line copy match the watches actually pictured; never promise "six" with fewer shown.
- [CELEBRITY COLLECTION] Before finalising, check headlines are 2–6 words, footers fit one line, and closing takes have a clear point within 15 words.
- [WATCH EXPLAINED] Before rendering, count each slide body against the 15-word limit and paraphrase only claims the cited source actually makes.
- [WATCH EXPLAINED] Before finalising, make the caption's call-to-action match the final slide's CTA word for word, so both ask for the same action.
- [WATCH EXPLAINED] Use only a permitted photo that visibly shows the exact model named in the story; otherwise use a text-free layout.
- [WATCH EXPLAINED] Give every slide a distinct claim and reason; never repeat one claim across slides, and merge slides that would.
- [WATCH EXPLAINED] Before naming a source in a caption or slide, open that page directly; cite only sources actually read, not ones seen in search snippets.
- [WATCH EXPLAINED] Remove decorative arrows, circles and other stray shapes from covers and slides unless the chosen template requires them; owner rules count them as defects.
- [WATCH EXPLAINED] Place the cover photo low enough that the tag and headline sit in a clear band above the dial, leaving every numeral visible.
- [WATCH EXPLAINED] Avoid deictic words like 'this one' or 'here' unless the slide's image shows the referent; otherwise name the watch outright.
- [WATCH EXPLAINED] Give each explainer a sourced mechanism or reason step; if it is only history, title it as a history story.
- [WATCH EXPLAINED] Make each slide label match what the slide does; use 'ANSWER' only when answering a question the post has just posed.
- [WATCH EXPLAINED] Before finalising, self-score the post against the 25-point checklist and fix weak gates; approval needs at least 17.
- [CELEBRITY] Count each slide body against the 15-word limit before rendering; trim any over-long body to 15 words or fewer.
- [CELEBRITY] Celebrity portrait covers need the gold watch medallion inset showing the featured watch, placed clear of the headline block, with no arrows.
- [WATCH EXPLAINED] Each mechanism step slide must show a permitted photo of the watch or part it describes; never leave steps as plain text on black.
- [WATCH EXPLAINED] Price or comparison slides need at least two sourced watches, each with price, date and photo; otherwise merge the content into another slide.
- [WATCH EXPLAINED] Keep each footer to one line by counting its characters; put long source dates in the caption, not the footer.
- [WATCH EXPLAINED] After deleting or reordering any slide, renumber the page counters and remove source footers from slides that no longer cite them.
- [WATCH EXPLAINED] Name in cover tag and sub-line only watches visibly pictured on the cover; mention other models in later slides that show their photo.
