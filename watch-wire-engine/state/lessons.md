# Lessons from the approver
The editor reads this before every post. Written automatically from rejections; newest last.

- [LISTICLE] Before rendering, confirm every slide has a validated licensed photo in the same style; if one is missing or boxed, swap in a different watch.
- [LISTICLE] Before drafting, list at least one more qualifying candidate than the item count, each with a dated price in the band and a usable photo; drop the rest.
- [LISTICLE] If a candidate fails the price band, date or photo check during drafting, replace it from official sources before submitting; never submit fewer items than the format requires.
- [LISTICLE] After any failed attempt, log which check each candidate failed; widen brands or models for the next try instead of repeating the same list.
- [LISTICLE] Each slide's footer must name only that slide's own source, matching the retailer or brand beside its price or claim.
- [LISTICLE] Before drafting, check that the highest price in the band is at most 2x the lowest; swap out any outlier watch.
- [LISTICLE] Verify each listicle price on the brand's official page or two fetched retailer listings; search snippets and HTTP errors don't count.
- [LISTICLE] Before submitting, register each photo in manifest.json with its real rights holder and show the dial; apply all fixes and rerun checks.
- [LISTICLE] Don't end a run at research. Keep going through drafting, rendering and checks until a complete draft is ready to submit.
- [LISTICLE] Before submitting, confirm every slide and caption is drafted and rendered; a research summary alone is not a deliverable.
- [STORY BEHIND] Before drafting, check the posted-story records for the same story ID or subject, and pick a different story if one already ran.
- [STORY BEHIND] Name the draft folder and ID from the final story slug after drafting, and confirm they match the slide content before submitting.
- [STORY BEHIND] Count words in each beat slide's body text before rendering; trim to the limit instead of shrinking type or overflowing.
- [STORY BEHIND] Rerun the posted-story duplicate check right before submitting, since another story may have run during drafting.
- [STORY BEHIND] Plan the slide count before drafting; if dropping an unverified claim leaves fewer than 7 slides, verify another beat from a second source.
- [STORY BEHIND] Before rendering, map where the watch dial sits in each photo and keep the cover headline and every beat's text in clear space.
- [STORY BEHIND] Map a watch-bearing photo to slides 1, 3, 4 and 6 before drafting; reject any image where no watch or wrist is visible.
- [STORY BEHIND] Leave no empty band on the cover: size headline and image together to fill the slide, then centre them as one block before rendering.
- [STORY BEHIND] Brighten every photo until the watch dial reads clearly before rendering; use a brighter crop, never a dark overlay.
- [STORY BEHIND] Check each slide's photo shows the exact model or event its text names; if the credit is for another subject, swap the image.
