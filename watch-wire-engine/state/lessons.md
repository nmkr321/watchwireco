# Lessons from the approver
The editor reads this before every post. Written automatically from rejections; newest last.

- [LISTICLE] After any slide or caption edit, re-render every preview and delete previews for removed slides so previews match the project's slide count and CTA.
- [LISTICLE] Use only adjectives and style claims a fetched source states; otherwise cut them, leaving price, date and spec facts only.
- [LISTICLE] Before rendering, produce research.json with research-agent origin, browser trace, specialist analysis and governance ledgers, then run validate and clear every error.
- [LISTICLE] Run validate after research, before drafting slides; if it lists missing records, generate each record from the fetched sources and re-run it.
- [WOULD YOU RATHER] Before reporting a post ready, confirm its project folder exists under `Content Creation/projects/` and passes validate. Fix any failure first.
- [WOULD YOU RATHER] If the project folder can't be created or validated, rebuild it from the idea's JSON instead of declaring the post ready.
- [WOULD YOU RATHER] Choose watches absent from posts in the last 7 days, with fetched prices on the same date within 10%.
- [ALL] Since 2026-11-21 the renderer removes plain white, cream or grey studio backgrounds from watch photos automatically and the cream panel is gone, so official product photos on light backgrounds are fine to use. Don't reject or skip a watch for its studio background; only avoid photos with busy scene backgrounds.
- [WOULD YOU RATHER] If a draft is stopped because a photo can't meet the template, swap in a different item with a compliant photo instead of re-rendering the same images.
- [WOULD YOU RATHER] Before choosing a pair, list at least three photo-compliant candidates per price tier. If one fails the photo or price check, pick the next from that list.
- [WOULD YOU RATHER] If no pair fits the same-date 10% price window, widen the search to other library dates or brands, then choose the closest compliant pair.
- [CELEBRITY] Use only stories whose full text loads from the source and that a second independent outlet also reports; drop paywalled or unconfirmed ones.
- [CELEBRITY] Skip any celebrity who is a current brand ambassador or paid partner of the watch's maker; choose a non-endorsing wearer.
- [CELEBRITY] Name a watch reference only when a fetched source states it; if the source is silent or blocked, pick another story.
- [CELEBRITY] If the best lead fails the checks, search further celebrity stories until one passes, rather than submitting the closest weak lead.
- [CELEBRITY] Confirm the celebrity is photographed wearing the watch at a named event, using a fetched photo caption or article, before drafting.
- [CELEBRITY] Drop leads whose story or event date is more than 7 days before today, and check dates against a fetched page.
- [WOULD YOU RATHER] Before submitting, check each slide against the approver's recurring rejections in state/lessons.md and fix matches first.
