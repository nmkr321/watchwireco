# Lessons from the approver
The editor reads this before every post. Written automatically from rejections; newest last.

- [WATCH EXPLAINED] Give every explainer slide a permitted photo of the watch, dial or movement; never leave a text-only slide on black.
- [WATCH EXPLAINED] Show every watch the recipe names, each on its own photo slide with its official brand price.
- [EVENT SPOTTED] Use only sources you could fully open; treat paywalled, redirected or blocked pages as unverified and never name a watch from them.
- [EVENT SPOTTED] If a permitted person's searches show only older sightings, move to the next permitted person and search again.
- [CELEBRITY COLLECTION] Inspect each photo on the dark template for white fades or stray boxes before rendering; replace any flawed image with a clean one.
- [CELEBRITY COLLECTION] Give each slide a different permitted photo; if a watch lacks a distinct official or licensed photo, drop its slide instead of repeating one.
- [CELEBRITY COLLECTION] Before rendering, check every slide's photo rights in assets/manifest.json; replace any image not marked official or licensed, or drop that slide.
- [STORY BEHIND] Give every slide a different licensed or official photo background; never leave a text-only slide on black.
- [STORY BEHIND] Use the source's exact verb for each event (created, unveiled, introduced), and make the cover photo show the object the story describes.
- [WATCH EXPLAINED] Use cover photos that show the explained mechanism itself; never substitute an unrelated object, even one that looks similar.
- [WATCH EXPLAINED] Check each slide's mechanism claim against the named watch's movement type, such as quartz or mechanical, before writing it.
- [EVENT SPOTTED] Sightings from the last 30 days are events; the article's date gives the year. If one person fails, take the next name the search found.
- [CELEBRITY COLLECTION] A person's collection = watches named by any trusted outlet or the person's own posts, last 12 months; missing price → "Price on request".
- [CELEBRITY COLLECTION] Person photos: Getty (photo.js credited) or any source in permissions.json; watch photos: the brand's official image.
- [WATCH EXPLAINED] Name only colours, sizes and features that a slide shows; CTA and caption last line must repeat slide wording, not add new details.
- [WATCH EXPLAINED] Don't repeat a word or fact in a slide body that its headline already states; give each slide one new fact.
- [NEW RELEASES] Match every slide's photo to the feature its text names (dial, bezel, wrist); view each image before assigning it.
- [NEW RELEASES] Keep source names out of slide body text; put each source beside its claim in the caption.
- [CELEBRITY] Make the cover the strongest permitted person photo with the watch in the circle; use a different portrait on later slides.
- [CELEBRITY] Print a year on a slide only if the source text or article date states it; otherwise leave the year out.
