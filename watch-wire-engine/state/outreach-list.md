# Who to ask for photo permission (24 Nov 2026)

What to ask for: "permission to use your press / media images on Instagram (@watchwireco), credited as you specify."
When someone says yes, tell Claude: who, what it covers, the exact credit line, and the date. Keep the email on your computer.

## Brand press offices (easiest; most have a media/press page on their website)
Ambassador photos (celebrities wearing the watch) come from these too.
1. Omega (James Bond, George Clooney)
2. Jaeger-LeCoultre (Robert Pattinson)
3. Rolex (Roger Federer and many sports ambassadors)

13. GPHG, the watch awards (gphg.org)
14. Christie's (press.christies.com) and Phillips: auction watch photos

## Celebrities and teams (slow, but worth a try)
A celebrity can only allow photos they own (their own posts). Red-carpet and game photos belong to the photographer or agency.
1. Robert Pattinson: his publicist
2. Harry Kane: his management, and Bayern Munich's media office
3. Mark Zuckerberg: Meta press office
4. Ryan Reynolds: his management (Maximum Effort)
5. Michael Jordan / Jordan Brand: Nike press office
6. Jay-Z: Roc Nation press
7. Cristiano Ronaldo: CR7 management
8. Kevin O'Leary: his team (he answers fans and media often)
9. Tom Brady: his management
10. Ivory Coast football federation (fif.ci): Yan Diomande photos
11. Argentina's football association (afa.com.ar): Messi and team photos

Names picked from what the top watch pages posted most this month and the trace in `photo-source-trace.md`.
