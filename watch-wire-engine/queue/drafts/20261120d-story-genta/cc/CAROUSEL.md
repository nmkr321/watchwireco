## Caption

One man designed the Audemars Piguet Royal Oak and the Patek Philippe Nautilus: Gérald Genta.

Genta was born in Geneva in 1931 and died in 2011. He was inspired by vintage diving helmets for the Royal Oak, and Patek Philippe introduced the Nautilus in 1976, designed by him. He also designed IWC's Ingenieur, and Cartier asked him to redesign the Pasha around 1985.

Source: Sotheby's, Wikipedia. Photo credits: Wikimedia Commons: Royal Oak ref. 15202 by OpaleHorse (CC BY-SA 4.0); Nautilus 5711 by Patek Philippe SA (CC BY-SA 4.0); Gérald Genta by Unknown author (CC BY-SA 3.0).

Sotheby's and Wikipedia both name him as designer; both describe the Royal Oak's diving-helmet inspiration. Sources disagree on the Royal Oak launch year, so no year is claimed for it.
