# Watch Wire formats (short brief for the editor and approver)

Short version of the brand guide and playbook. The full brand guide is
`The-Watch-Wire-Brand-Guide.md`; this brief must match it.

**Voice:** confident, insider, concise and checked. "Swiss watch exports rose 9.1% in
October. Fifth month in a row." Never hype, emoji headlines or "buy now".

**Value test (every post):** why would someone save, share or comment? It must be useful (a clear use or buyer: "watches for a suit", "first luxury watch under $5K", "travel GMTs"), timely (news, an auction, a release; seasonal angles only occasionally) or interactive (pick, vote, build). Lists and collections need a niche: one use case and a tight price band (roughly within 2x, e.g. $3K–$6K), never random watches from $9K to $82K.

**Always:** a source for every number and a date for every price; the source named for
single-source facts ("per GQ"), never "reportedly"; one idea per slide; a topic tag on the cover; "spotted
wearing" unless ownership is confirmed; say whether auction prices include fees.
**Never:** price predictions, investment advice, clickbait the slides don't pay
off, reposting other creators' slides or photos, a press photo presented as ours.
**CTA:** calm and direct, one action ("Vote in the comments." / "Follow @watchwireco
for daily watch news.").

|---|---|---|---|

| NEW RELEASES | Brand + model + the one new thing | 6–8 | cover (press photo) → photo → specs table → price/date → why it matters → cta |
| AUCTIONS | Price shock: "$5.6M For A Paul Newman" | 6–8 | cover → hammer price (say if fees included) → the lot → record context → cta |
| MARKET | Key number, stated plainly | 5–6 | cover → number → table → caveat → cta |
| CELEBRITY | Name + watch + twist / price | 5–7 | cover (brand campaign photo of them wearing it, else their free photo) → A-spotted (person + inset watch) → the watch (A-photo) → price/rarity → cta |
| CELEBRITY COLLECTION | "[Name]'s watch collection is getting interesting" / "Inside [Name]'s collection" | 6–9 | cover (portrait + one watch in the gold circle) → one slide per watch (official photo, name, price or rarity) → our take → Your call: "which one would you take?" → cta. Owner's favourite example: @nauvetimepieces Ryan Reynolds / Zuckerberg posts (3–5K likes on 9K followers). One trusted outlet naming the watches is enough. |
| TOP 5 THIS WEEK | "Top 5 watch releases this week" | 7 | cover → 5 releases, one per slide (official photo, price) → Your call: "which one wins?" → cta. Sunday roundup. |
| EVENT SPOTTED | "Watches Spotted At The 2026 F1 Las Vegas Grand Prix" (a real event from the last ~10 days) | 6–9 | cover (A-cover photo = best Getty event photo, watches = 3-5 of the watches seen, line1 = event) → one A-spotted per celebrity (Getty photo of them at the event + their watch in the medallion; name, watch, price) → Your call: "best wrist of the weekend?" → cta. Getty/brand photos via `PH credited`. Owner's favourite (2026-11-25). |
| WATCH EXPLAINED | "How A Minute Repeater Actually Works" / "What A Tourbillon Does" / "GMT vs World Timer, Explained", ideally hooked to a recent release that has it | 6–8 | cover (owner 2026-11-25: the MOVEMENT or mechanism photo, big, the most striking picture in the post; the watch comes later) → what it is in one line → how it works in 2-3 simple steps (A-photo of the movement/dial, brand press images) → which watches have it, with prices → why collectors care → cta. Plain words, no jargon. |
| WOULD YOU RATHER (cover tag SAME MONEY when prices match; ONLY on owner request) | Binary: "$11K. Which One?" | 4–5 | cover → A-wyr with both prices (within ~10% of each other, same date) → one A-photo per option → "Vote in the comments." cta |
| BUILD YOUR COLLECTION (ONLY on owner request) | "Build Your 3-Watch Collection. $25K Total." | 5–6 | cover → one A-pick3 per tier (label "Pick 1 · up to $6,000", headline = watch type, 3 options A/B/C, each under the tier cap) → cta "Comment your collection: 1B, 2A, 3C". Viewer builds it; never pre-pick. Tiers are different types (dress, sports/diver, chronograph/GMT, field, gold/leather), not all steel sports. Vary caps and types per post. |
| STORY BEHIND (Template D, images through to the last beats) | Tension: "One Man Designed The Royal Oak And The Nautilus." | 7–9 | alternate two kinds: a person who changed watchmaking (Genta, Wilsdorf, Daniels, Breguet, Dufour…) or the story behind one famous watch (Tank, Moonwatch, Paul Newman Daytona…). D-cover → one fact per D-body beat → payoff → cta |
| STORY / HISTORY (Template D) | Tension: "Rolex Started As A London Import Company." | 7–10 | D-cover → D-body beats (one fact each) → payoff → cta |
| LISTICLE (ONLY on owner request) | "Every X, Explained" / "N watches that…" | 8–10 | cover → one item per slide → cta |

Prices: current US retail from the brand or an official retailer, dated ("US retail, Nov 2026"); never grey-market prices as retail.
Photos: from `watch-wire-engine/src/photo.js` only (see the editor). Every photo slide carries its `credit`.

Never post the same format twice in a row; the next post must be a different type.

Template A for everything except STORY/HISTORY and STORY BEHIND, which use D. Never mix them.
Owner's direction (2026-11-25): celebrities and events first, then watches explained, new releases and new lines, features when relevant. Covers are photo-led: a full-bleed person, wrist or event photo, or several watches together; never words on a black screen.
History works only with a tension hook. A date plus a fact on its own flops.
