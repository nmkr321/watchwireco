# Brand guide

The Watch Wire · @watchwireco · v1.0 · November 2026
Full visual kit: watch-wire-kit/index.html (published page: https://claude.ai/artifact/XoLZHHXqZHgCWanUjXiCWD)

## Brand name

The Watch Wire (Instagram: @watchwireco)

## Audience

Luxury watch enthusiasts and collectors, from people saving for a first Rolex
to seasoned collectors. They follow brands, releases, auctions, prices and
celebrity watches, and want the news fast, accurate and in one place.

What it is: a daily luxury watch news page covering releases, auctions, market
moves, celebrity collections and brand stories.
What it is not: a dealer, paid hype, price predictions, or reposts of other
creators' content.
The promise: every post has a real source, every number is checked, and rumours
are labelled as rumours.

## Voice

- Confident
- Insider
- Concise and checked

Write like this: "Swiss watch exports rose 9.1% in October. Fifth month in a row."

Do not sound like this: "The watch market is EXPLODING 🚀 buy now before it doubles!!"

## Colors

- Primary background: Jet #0B0B0C
- Text: Ivory #F4EFE6
- Accent (signal only): Champagne #C8A96A
- Labels and sources: Smoke #9B968C
- Panels: Ink #17171A

Ratio: jet + ivory 90%, smoke + ink 8%, champagne 2%. One champagne moment per
slide: the topic tag, the second headline line, the key number, WIRE in the
wordmark, one key fact in a story sentence, or the follow button. Never
champagne backgrounds, body text, text on ivory, or decorative borders.

## Typography

- News headlines, tags, wordmark: Oswald 700, uppercase (fallback: Arial Narrow, Impact, sans-serif)
- Story headlines and story sentences: Poppins 700 / 600, title case (fallback: Helvetica Neue, Arial)
- Monogram, story wordmark, "or" on Would You Rather: Playfair Display 600 (fallback: Georgia, serif)
- Body, labels, sources: Inter 400 / 600 (fallback: Helvetica, Arial)

All four are free Google Fonts.

Sizes on a 1080 × 1350 slide: number 240–260px; news cover headline 96–120px;
story cover headline 96–104px; inside headline 72–84px; story sentence 52–56px;
body 32–36px; labels 18–24px with +0.16em letter-spacing.

## Logo rules

Text-only; no drawn logo file.

- Wordmark: "THE WATCH WIRE" in Oswald 700, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.06em.
  "THE WATCH" in ivory, "WIRE" in champagne. Used top-left on news slides,
  32px on a 1080-wide slide, minimum 18px. Below 18px use the handle @WATCHWIRECO.
- Monogram: "WW" in Playfair Display 600, ivory. Used top-centre on story
  slides and as the profile picture (ivory WW on jet, 400 × 400).
- Story slides use the serif wordmark "THE WATCH WIRE" in Playfair Display,
  all ivory, bottom-centre.
- Clearspace equals the height of the W on all sides.
- Never: champagne on the whole wordmark, mixed case, stretching, or placing
  the mark over a busy part of a photo. Never both marks at the top of one slide.

## Imagery

- Use: official brand press images, auction-house lot photos, event press photos, and the user's own photos. Clean product shots on dark or neutral backgrounds for news slides.
- Treatment: News (Template A) puts the photo on the top 2/3 and fades it into jet from 40% down. Story (Template D) uses a full-bleed photo under a 60% jet overlay (80% on inside slides). Every slide with a photo carries a credit line "Image: [Brand or source]" in smoke, 16px, bottom-right.
- Permission: press photos are registered as "permission-needed" in the asset manifest. Own photos may be "owned".
- Avoid: screenshots of other creators' posts, images with someone else's watermark, flipped, recoloured or AI-edited watches, stock photos, busy collages.

## Visual direction

Black-and-champagne luxury news, in two templates that share one palette.
Template A (News) is for news, releases, auctions, market data and Would You
Rather: photo fading into jet, champagne topic tag, uppercase Oswald headline
with a champagne second line, wordmark top-left, handle top-right, source and
page number along the bottom. Template D (Story) is for brand histories and
longer stories: full-bleed dark photo, WW monogram top-centre, big centred
Poppins headline in title case, circle arrow, serif wordmark at the bottom.
Never mix A and D inside one carousel. Every carousel ends with the shared
closing slide: monogram, one line, champagne "FOLLOW @WATCHWIRECO" button.
Spacing uses an 8pt grid with a 48px margin on every side.

## Content rules

Always:

- State facts plainly, as statements. Never "per GQ", "according to", "reportedly" or "as reported in" in slides or caption text; every source goes in the "Source:" line at the bottom of the caption (and the slide footer). Owner 2026-11-26.
- Give a date for every price (in the Source line).
- If a fact can't be stated confidently, leave it out.
- Keep one idea per slide.
- Put the topic tag on every cover: NEWS, NEW RELEASES, AUCTIONS, MARKET, CELEBRITY, WOULD YOU RATHER or STORY.
- Say "spotted wearing" for celebrities unless ownership is confirmed. Say whether auction prices include fees.

Never:

- Make price predictions or give investment advice.
- Use clickbait the slides don't pay off, or emojis in headlines.
- Repost another creator's slides or photos.
- Present a press photo as our own.

## CTA style

Calm and direct, one action. Examples: "Vote in the comments." "Swipe for the
full list." "Follow @watchwireco for daily watch news."
